Current Research

Exploring the frontiers of mechanical engineering through innovative research projects

Autonomous Robotic Systems for Manufacturing

Ongoing • 2023-2026 • NSF Funded
[Research Image Placeholder]

This project focuses on developing next-generation autonomous robotic systems capable of adaptive manufacturing processes. Our research integrates advanced control algorithms, machine learning techniques, and real-time sensor feedback to create robots that can autonomously adjust their operations based on changing manufacturing conditions and requirements.

Key innovations include predictive maintenance algorithms, collaborative human-robot interfaces, and adaptive quality control systems that ensure consistent production quality while maximizing efficiency and safety in industrial environments.

Robotics Machine Learning Manufacturing Control Systems

Smart Materials for Aerospace Applications

Ongoing • 2024-2027 • NASA Collaboration
[Research Image Placeholder]

Investigation of programmable materials and metamaterials with tunable mechanical properties for aerospace applications. This research explores shape-memory alloys, piezoelectric composites, and bio-inspired materials that can adapt their properties in response to environmental conditions.

Applications include morphing wing structures, adaptive thermal protection systems, and lightweight structural components that can optimize performance across different flight conditions while maintaining structural integrity and safety standards.

Smart Materials Aerospace Metamaterials Composites

Sustainable Energy Systems Integration

Ongoing • 2023-2025 • DOE Grant
[Research Image Placeholder]

Development of integrated sustainable energy systems that combine renewable energy sources with advanced storage and distribution technologies. Our research focuses on optimizing the mechanical design of wind turbines, solar tracking systems, and energy storage mechanisms for maximum efficiency and reliability.

The project includes novel approaches to mechanical energy harvesting, advanced materials for energy storage systems, and intelligent control systems that can predict and adapt to changing energy demands and environmental conditions.
